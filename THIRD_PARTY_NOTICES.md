### OpenStreetMap via Overture Maps (water, buildings, roads)
`build_terrain.py --layers water,buildings,roads` reads Overture Maps `base/water`, `base/land_use` (only to leave
military areas out), `buildings/building`, `buildings/building_part` and `transportation/segment` and writes
`water.png`, `buildings.bin`, `footprints.png` and `roads.bin`. These files are a Derivative Database of
OpenStreetMap and are licensed under the Open Database License 1.0
(https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/); anything that shows them must credit
"© OpenStreetMap contributors, Overture Maps Foundation" (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The building
layer also holds Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints where OpenStreetMap has none (Microsoft's own licence is
CDLA-Permissive-2.0; Overture distributes them under ODbL, and they are mixed into one ODbL layer here). This
applies to `tests/data/uskudar_small/` (those four files) in this repository and to every package built with these
layers; the other layers keep their own notices, and the code stays MIT. Screenshots and videos of the terrain are
Produced Works: the credit lines are enough. `docs/city/*.jpg` show them on screen.

## Specifications and algorithms (no code copied)
- Building heights and roofs follow the OpenStreetMap Simple 3D Buildings tagging as carried by Overture
  (`height`, `min_height`, `roof_shape`, `roof_height`, parts); the ground model is our own (a mask-weighted image
  pyramid fill), not a published filter.
- `tools/dted.py` follows the public DTED specification MIL-PRF-89020B; GDAL's DTED driver is used only as a test
  oracle.
- `GeoMath` UTM follows C. F. F. Karney, *Transverse Mercator with an accuracy of a few nanometers* (2011). The golden
  file `tests/golden/utm_mgrs_cases.txt` was generated with PROJ (pyproj) and the Python `mgrs` package (MIT,
  Howard Butler); `mgrs` is not a dependency.
- `GeoVisibility.viewshed` implements the R2 sweep described by W. R. Franklin and C. Ray (1994). GRASS GIS
  `r.viewshed` (GPL) was background reading only.
